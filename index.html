How to play

One catches. One returns.

A cannonball entering either portal comes out of the other. Defeat the cannons before five shots hit your village.

  1. 1

    Catch. A pale mark shows where the next shot will pass. Move a portal there to catch it.

  2. 2

    Aim. Point the other portal at a cannon. The dotted line shows where the ball will go.

  3. 3

    Go around cover. Move the exit portal sideways when a shutter blocks your shot.

Mouse & keyboard

Move the pointer to aim. Drag either portal to move it.
Q / E switch portals · Scroll or ↑ / ↓ for depth
Space pause · R restart

VR hands

Your palms control the portals. Lift both hands to start or play again. Keep both hands in view.
